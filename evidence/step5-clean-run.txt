--- Why three runs and not one -----------------------------------------------

The system was live throughout, beating once a minute. The verifier pulls the log
over SSH and reads the chain locally, and those two reads are not simultaneous.
A heartbeat landing between them would produce a single spurious alert on the
newest sequence -- DELETION if the chain read came second, UNWITNESSED if it came
first -- from the race rather than from any fault.

Three runs across eight seconds, all reporting the same boundary sequence and all
clean, distinguishes a genuine result from a race artefact. This is inherent to
verifying a live log rather than a defect, and it is the reason the run was
repeated rather than reported from a single pass.
