================================================================================
WHAT THE DIGEST RECOMPUTATION DOES AND DOES NOT PROVE
================================================================================

The check recomputes every stored digest from its record and compares. 21 of 21
matched.

It imports the same canonical module the agent used, so it is not an independent
check on the serialisation itself. What it establishes is that the digest stored
beside each record is the digest of that record under the shared definition --
which is exactly what the verifier will test from the ledger host. A mismatch
here would mean the write path was broken before the ledger was involved at all.

--- The decomposition, because a naive reading gets this badly wrong -----------

Someone reading total_us max 31158 will conclude hash-at-write costs 31ms per
event. It does not. The hash is 570µs of that at worst and 131µs typically. What
costs is durability, and it splits in two:

  The log write and its fsync (~3.1ms median) is NOT overhead attributable to
  this design. Any audit logger whose records must survive a power cut pays it.

  The state-file persist (~3.5ms median) IS this design's addition: an atomic
  temp-write, fsync and rename per event so the sequence is recoverable. It
  roughly doubles the fsync cost and is the largest attributable overhead in the
  system.

  The hash itself is ~1.7%, most of which is Python interpreter time rather than
  SHA-256.

The defensible claim is therefore that hashing in the write path is not the cost
-- it is under 2% -- while sequence persistence is, and that is addressable
without touching the hash-at-write property.

--- Three caveats, and they are not decorative ---------------------------------

1. n=22 is a smoke test, not a measurement. T6 needs orders of magnitude more
   samples. Nothing here belongs in a limitations section as "measured".

2. Variance is severe: 5984µs to 31159µs, a 5.2x spread across 22 samples. Report
   percentiles, never a mean. The mean (9110) sits well above the median (6922)
   because of a single outlier.

3. push_us is meaningless in this run. Under --no-push it measures a branch not
   taken -- 1-4µs of bookkeeping. It is not a network cost and must not be
   presented as one.

digest_before_write_us is reported above purely as the log-write-and-fsync
interval. It is not offered as evidence for claim 1 anywhere.
